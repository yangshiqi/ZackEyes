## Behavior to preserve

- Notch answers are opt-in and disabled by default, limited to supported Claude questions; unsupported/expired requests fall back to the terminal.
- SSH is observation only, up to eight hosts. The remote Swift 6 helper must be installed separately; key authentication and host keys must already work. Remote sessions do not enter local Usage/quota/journal data.
- Demo actions never send approvals or answers to real agents; real attention returns to live sessions.
- Usage settles local completed days through yesterday after 03:00 while running, with startup catch-up. Initial automatic coverage is the last three complete days, not all historical transcripts.
- Settings reads small saved archives. Earlier history requires explicit import; committed progress survives pause/restart. Previous JSONL history and source transcripts remain unchanged.
- Agent-hours are estimates, and concurrent sessions add hours. Skipped invalid/oversized observations are reported; nonzero statistics do not prove complete settlement.
- No prompts, replies or tool arguments are saved in Usage. Private database and sidecar permissions remain enforced.
